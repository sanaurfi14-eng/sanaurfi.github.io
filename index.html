About me

I learn by doing.
Then I teach it.

"I love trying new things, experimenting, failing, learning, and getting things right."

With a BBA in Marketing and a brain wired for creativity, I found my home in L&D — the sweet spot between storytelling, design, and behaviour change. At JazzWorld, I've built game-based e-learnings from scratch, led large-scale learning initiatives, and brought world-class speakers in front of hundreds of curious colleagues.
